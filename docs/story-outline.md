# Area Boy: story outline (APPROVED)

You approved the backstory, names and secret endings, and that skins can be
re-toned. All people, parties, cults, agencies and brands are invented.

## Setting

**Iyana-Wahala Bus Stop**, Lagos: a junction under a flyover, a market behind
it, a motor park beside it, rich compounds two streets away. Everything in the
game happens around it:

- **The Front Stop**: the busy lay-by. Baba Ticket's people control it.
- **The Back Side**: the worst corner of the stop. This is where the player starts.
- **Under-Bridge**: Scatter's territory, plus the hideouts.
- **Ọlọ́ja Market**: container stalls, Ada's shop, Baba Radio's stall.
- **Zone 5 Estate**: rich compounds behind high walls. This is where the begging happens.
- **The Roundabout**: the rallies and the final act.

## The protagonist

- **Name and nickname:** chosen by the player. Until he earns a street name,
  everyone calls him **"JJC"** (Johnny Just Come).
- **Backstory (please confirm):** He's 24, from the small town of Ìlú-Àlàáfíà,
  and has an OND in Business Admin. He got a job as a "field marketer" for
  **Double-Double Investment Club**, a fictional Ponzi scheme, and he believed
  in it. He talked his mother and her market-women's association into
  investing **₦850,000**. Then Double-Double "migrated to a new server" and
  disappeared. The women now hold his mother responsible. He can't go home
  until the money is paid back, so he took the night bus to Lagos with ₦2,300
  and a phone on 3% battery.
- **The constant pressure:** **Mama** calls. **Iya Bọ́sẹ̀**, the leader of the
  market women, sends voice notes. Each act has a repayment deadline. Paying
  or missing it changes later scenes and opens or closes the "Go Home" ending.

## Recurring side characters (their small stories react to your choices)

- **Driver Bíọ́dún** drives an old danfo called *"God's Time Is The Best"*. He's the first defaulter, and he becomes a friend or an enemy.
- **Mama Put (Mama Nkechi)** runs the food buka. She gives credit if your goodwill is high.
- **Mallam Garba** is the tea and bread seller who sees everything. He sells rumours.
- **Baba Radio** has a radio stall in the market. Every economic shock reaches the street through him.
- **Prophet Fire-Fire** is a street preacher who prophesies about the player, and the prophecies come true depending on your flags.
- **Iya Keke (Mrs. Folake)** is a keke driver who refuses to pay any agbero.
- **Mustapha "Formula One"** is an okada rider and getaway driver.
- **Kelvin "Crypto"** sells "forex signals". He's a mirror of the player's own Double-Double past.
- **Nurse Rónkẹ́** at **General Hospital Iyana-Wahala** appears in every collapse or defeat scene and remembers you.

## Acts and chapters

### Act 1: "The Spot" (tutorial through story)

1. **Ch1 "JJC".** You arrive at 5:40 a.m. A fake conductor scams you, and
   Professor saves you. Baba Ticket gives you the Back Side on trial, with a
   quota of ₦15,000 a day. First fee collection. Driver Bíọ́dún runs, so you
   chase him. First energy crash, and Professor buys you your first
   "Agbára Bitters".
2. **Ch2 "Rules of the Road".** Professor's rules. You meet Ada and Sunday.
   Scatter's boys "test" you. Mama's first phone call and the first repayment
   deadline. This is the first full free-roam day.
3. **Ch3 "Fuel Don Cost".** Baba Radio announces a fuel price hike. The drivers
   protest and Baba Ticket raises your quota. **First big choice:** raise fees
   (drivers get angry, the Chairman is happy) or absorb the loss (goodwill goes
   up and your debt hurts more).

### Act 2: "Rising"

4. **Ch4 "Conductor".** Sunday gets arrested, so you conduct Bíọ́dún's danfo
   for a day with named passengers. Scatter extorts the bus at Under-Bridge,
   which leads to a fight.
5. **Ch5 "Rain and Raid".** The first rain. The LETS Task Force raids the stop:
   hide, run, fight or bribe. Commander Bassey's story starts.
6. **Ch6 "Settlement".** The fixer offers the first rally job. You can do it,
   fake it, or warn the other side. Notoriety rises.
7. **Ch7 "Shell Knock".** Chuks recruits you. A begging run goes wrong when you
   beg from the wrong person (Owó Ńlá's nephew or Bassey off duty). The Hyenas
   beat Professor. **Choice:** join the Tortoises for protection, or stay
   independent.

### Act 3: "The Brotherhood" (two versions)

- **Cult path.** 8. Initiation with coded questions; wrong answers get you
  attacked. 9. Climb the ranks through fights or money, and get cult help in
  raids and collections. 10. War with the Hyenas, runner escapes, and the
  loyalty test: Sunday is a Hyena. Do you warn him, spare him or give him up?
- **Independent path.** 8. "Ọmọ Onílẹ̀". You set up a drivers' cooperative with
  Ada, Bíọ́dún and Iya Keke. 9. Both cults lean on you, and raids get harder
  with no protection. 10. Sunday asks for help getting out of the Hyenas.
- Both paths end with **Professor's fate**.

### Act 4: "The Reckoning"

11. **Ch11 "Election Season".** The biggest rally at the Roundabout. Settlement
    makes his final big offer.
12. **Ch12 "Operation Clean Sweep".** The Task Force crackdown and Bassey's
    endgame. Someone betrays you: Scatter, Settlement or Chuks, depending on
    your flags.
13. **Ch13 "Final Choice".** The ending is decided, then the epilogue plays.

## Ada: the player decides what she is

You approved that the relationship with Ada is up to the player and must
affect the game. It runs on the `friendBond` meter plus three path flags, and
the player chooses at three moments:

| Moment | Choice |
|---|---|
| Act 1 (Ch2) | First impressions: help her, flirt, or ignore her |
| Act 2 (Ch7) | Open up about the debt, or keep lying |
| Act 3 (Ch10) | **The question:** ask her out (needs bond ≥ 60), stay close friends, or walk away |

| Path | Flag | How it changes the game |
|---|---|---|
| Romance | `ada_romance` | She covers some of your debt payments if you stay honest. She leaves you if you lie about cult membership. The Hyenas can target her in the Act 3 war, which raises the stakes of the loyalty test. Her epilogue line changes in every ending, and Legit Businessman becomes "Iyana-Wahala Transport Ltd, owned by the Okafor family". |
| Close friend | `ada_friend` | She keeps your hustle savings safe from raids. She warns you before Task Force sweeps. In the Legit ending she becomes your business partner. |
| Walked away / estranged | `ada_estranged` | No credit at Ada's Corner, and the market gossips against you (community goodwill goes down). Go Home and Legit Businessman get harder. She has one last scene in Act 4 where you can reconcile. |

Low `friendBond` (under 20) at the Act 3 question sets `ada_estranged`
automatically, so ignoring her is also a choice.

## Meters and flags the story tracks

- **Relationships:** Chairman trust, Mentor bond, Friend bond (Ada), Community
  goodwill, Police heat, Cult loyalty, Rival cult hatred.
- **Personal:** money in three pockets (personal, hustle, cult), the Mama
  debt, energy (red eyes) and notoriety.
- **Callbacks:** for example, if you chased Bíọ́dún in Ch1, he refuses you a
  free ride in Ch4. If you let him go, he hides you in his bus during the Ch5
  raid. Every chapter should have at least two callbacks like this.

## Tone

Comedy first, pressure underneath. Pidgin and English. No real people, brands
or groups. Fights are cartoon-style. Drinks and "hard jokes" are abstract
energy items with invented names (for example *Agbára Bitters*, *Red-Eye
Tonic*, *Combat Zobo*, *Konji Cola*). A "hard joke" is a paid round of street
banter from **Comedian Ẹ̀wọ̀** that makes you laugh until your eyes go red.
